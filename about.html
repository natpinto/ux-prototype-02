Who We Are

A Team Rooted in the Pacific Northwest

Prizm was founded by four former colleagues with a simple goal in mind: to create a place where people are cared for and care about their work.

Together, we bring over 55 years of experience in helping communities grow, designing reliable infrastructure, and creating positive interactions with the built environment. Based in Bellevue, our team has spent that time working directly with municipalities, developers, and property owners across the Pacific Northwest, building the kind of local knowledge that only comes from years in the region.
