Mercer Island, WA

2026 Water System Improvements

Provided design and specifications for the replacement of a 65-year-old cast iron water main along 82nd Ave SE, Forest Ave SE, and W Mercer Pl. The improvements included the replacement of 1,000 linear feet of undersized 4- and 6-inch cast iron watermain with new 6- and 8-inch ductile iron pipe, replacement of PRV station F3-B, upgraded fire hydrants and new services to existing parcels.
